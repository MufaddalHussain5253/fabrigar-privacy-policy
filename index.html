1. Introduction

APPWERKS ("we", "us", or "our") operates the FabRigar mobile application ("FabRigar" or "the App").

FabRigar is designed to help fabrication businesses manage their projects, products, measurements, configurations, and related business information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

By using FabRigar, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account and Profile Information

When you create and use a FabRigar account, we may collect information that you provide, including:

  • Mobile phone number
  • Name
  • Shop name
  • State
  • District
  • City

Your mobile phone number is used for OTP-based authentication and to securely authenticate your FabRigar account.

2.2 Project and Business Information

FabRigar allows you to create and manage information related to your fabrication work. This may include:

  • Project names
  • Client information that you choose to enter
  • Project dates
  • Project status
  • Project location information that you manually enter
  • Product types
  • Product dimensions and measurements
  • Product configurations
  • Profile and section specifications
  • Glass-related configurations
  • Colour selections
  • Cutting and fabrication information
  • Remarks and other information you choose to enter into the App

We use this information to provide the core functionality of FabRigar.

2.3 Location Information

FabRigar does not use GPS or collect your device's precise location.

Location information such as state, district, city, or project location is collected only when you manually provide it through the App.

3. Information We Do Not Collect for Tracking or Advertising

FabRigar currently does not use:

  • Advertising SDKs
  • Analytics services
  • Crash-reporting services
  • Behavioural tracking SDKs
  • Advertising identifiers for targeted advertising

FabRigar does not sell your personal information to advertisers.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Create and authenticate your FabRigar account
  • Provide access to the App
  • Store and manage your profile
  • Create and manage projects
  • Store and manage product and fabrication information
  • Provide calculations, configurations, summaries, and other App functionality
  • Maintain and operate the FabRigar service
  • Respond to support or privacy-related requests
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service

We do not use your information for targeted advertising.

5. Authentication and Data Storage

Firebase Authentication

FabRigar uses Firebase Authentication to provide phone-number OTP authentication and securely authenticate users.

Your phone number is processed as part of the authentication process.

Supabase

FabRigar uses Supabase to store application data associated with your account, including profile, project, product, configuration, and related information.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate FabRigar, including authentication and database infrastructure providers such as Firebase and Supabase.

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, security, and safety of our users, our service, or others.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information stored and processed through FabRigar.

Data transmitted between the App and supported online services is protected using appropriate secure communication mechanisms.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain your account and application information for as long as necessary to provide FabRigar's services and maintain your account.

When you request deletion of your account, we will process the request and delete associated personal and application data, except where certain information must be retained because of a legal, security, fraud-prevention, or other legitimate requirement.

Any retained information will be handled only for the purpose for which it is required to be retained.

9. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your FabRigar account and associated data.

Deletion requests:
